Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026.

What this tool is

Gameplay Clips Poster is a private tool run by one person to upload short video clips to that person's own YouTube channel. Only the owner of the channel signs in to it.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to upload videos to that YouTube channel (the YouTube upload permission). It does not read the channel's videos, comments, subscribers, messages or any other Google account data.

What it stores

The sign-in key Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer, in its system keychain. It is used only to upload videos. It is never shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone.

What it collects from visitors

Nothing. This site sets no cookies and collects no personal data. The tool has no users other than its owner.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access deletes the tool's ability to upload; the stored key then stops working.

Contact

Questions: use the contact address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
